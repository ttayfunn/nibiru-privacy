Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nibiru: Gold of Earth ("Nibiru", "the game") handles information when you use the game.

Summary: Nibiru does not require a user account. Core game progress is stored locally on the device. The game uses third-party services for advertising, consent management and optional in-app purchases.

1. Data stored locally on your device

Nibiru may store gameplay information locally so your progress can be preserved. This may include:

  • Game progress, scores, unlocked stages, missions, achievements, upgrades and settings
  • Virtual currency balances such as Gold and Crystals
  • Locally stored purchase-delivery markers used to help prevent duplicate rewards
  • Whether permanent purchases, such as Remove Ads, have been applied

Nibiru does not currently operate its own user-account or cloud-save system.

2. Advertising and consent

Nibiru may display rewarded and interstitial advertisements using Google AdMob. Depending on region, consent choices, device settings and ad configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, app/device information, consent status, diagnostics and ad interaction data.

Google privacy information: Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Nibiru offers optional in-app purchases such as virtual currency, game packs and permanent ad removal. Payments are processed by Google Play on Android and Apple on iOS. Nibiru does not receive or store full payment-card details.

Nibiru may use RevenueCat to help manage purchase status and restoration. RevenueCat may process product identifiers, transaction information, app/device information and identifiers needed to provide purchase functionality.

RevenueCat privacy information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

The current game design does not require access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise GPS location for core gameplay. Haptic feedback may be used locally when enabled.

5. How information may be used

  • Provide rewarded and interstitial advertising
  • Manage advertising consent and privacy choices
  • Process, verify and restore in-app purchases
  • Prevent duplicate delivery of purchased content
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and diagnose technical problems
  • Comply with applicable legal and platform requirements

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate advertising and purchases, including Google/AdMob, Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data can generally be deleted by uninstalling Nibiru or clearing the app's storage/data in Android settings. Purchase, advertising, security or fraud-prevention records maintained by third-party providers may be retained according to their legal and service requirements.

For detailed deletion instructions, see the Nibiru Data Deletion Request page.

For privacy questions or requests concerning data under our control, email uforoom@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

Nibiru is not specifically designed or marketed for young children and does not require users to provide a name, email address, phone number or precise location through an account system.

9. International processing

Third-party providers used by the game may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms and applicable law.

10. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect app functionality and data under our control. No electronic storage or network transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its services or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
