Nibiru: Gold of Earth - Data Deletion Request

Developer: Tayfun Baş

This page explains how users of Nibiru: Gold of Earth can delete local game data or request deletion of data that may be associated with third-party game services.

No account is required. Nibiru: Gold of Earth does not currently provide user accounts. Most gameplay progress is stored locally on the device.

How to delete local game data

  1. Uninstall Nibiru: Gold of Earth from your device, or clear the app's storage/data from Android settings.
  2. This removes locally stored gameplay data such as progress, settings, local virtual-currency balances, mission progress, unlocks and locally stored purchase-delivery markers.

How to request deletion of service-related data

  1. Open the Google Play listing for Nibiru: Gold of Earth.
  2. Open the App support section and use the developer support email shown there.
  3. Use the subject line Nibiru Data Deletion Request.
  4. If your request concerns an in-app purchase, include enough information to identify the relevant record, such as the Google Play order ID. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details.
  5. We will review the request and delete, or request deletion of, data under our control where technically and legally possible.

Data that may be deleted

  • Local gameplay data stored by the app on the user's device
  • Locally stored transaction-delivery markers used to help prevent duplicate delivery of purchased virtual items
  • Data under the developer's direct control that can be reasonably linked to a valid deletion request

Data that may be retained

Certain records handled by Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat, AdMob or other service providers may be retained when required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, dispute-resolution or platform-compliance purposes. These providers may apply their own retention periods and deletion procedures.

Purchase records needed to verify completed transactions or prevent fraud may be retained for as long as required by the relevant platform or applicable law. Nibiru does not store full payment-card information.

Additional provider requests

Some data is controlled directly by third-party providers. Users may also need to submit deletion or privacy requests directly to Google, Apple or RevenueCat under those providers' own privacy procedures.
